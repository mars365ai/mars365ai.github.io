# Mars365 AI Website

This context describes the public website for Mars365 AI and the business concepts it presents to prospective customers.

## Language

**Company**:
The configurable public identity of the AI solutions provider represented by the website. Unconfirmed history, credentials, customer results, and contact details are not asserted as facts.

**Service**:
A capability the company offers, such as AI strategy, agent automation, or custom AI development. A service describes the kind of work available, not a packaged product.

**Solution**:
An illustrative application of AI to a business problem, describing the challenge, approach, and possible benefits. It is not a claim of a shipped product or customer deployment, and outcomes are not guaranteed.
_Avoid_: Product, case study

**Industry**:
A business sector used to frame its challenges and relevant AI opportunities, services, and illustrative solutions.

**Insight**:
An editorial article that explains an AI topic, practice, or opportunity for the site's business audience.

**Business inquiry**:
A prospective customer's request to discuss an AI need or potential engagement with the company.
_Avoid_: Contact form submission
